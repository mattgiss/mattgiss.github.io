Safe Drone Flying Spots Survey

SAFESPOTS-2026-10 · Delivered
Brighton, CO, Adams County, CO · Delivered 24 October 2026

Overview

Built for GIS 4690 at the University of Denver. A Survey123 form records recommended drone launch sites from personal flight history and field notes, including hazards at each site. All 14 sites sit in Class G airspace with no LAANC grid. The results are published through a read-only public view layer, a web map, and an Instant App with a sidebar for browsing the sites.

Processing

SoftwareArcGIS Survey123, ArcGIS Online, ArcGIS Instant Apps

Methods

  • Airspace check for each site (Class G, no LAANC grid)
  • Results published as a read-only public view layer
  • Web map and Instant App sidebar for browsing

Deliverables

  • Survey123 form and 14 survey results
  • Public web map
  • Instant App

Key Technical Challenge

Turning personal flight history into a consistent, repeatable survey that other pilots could use to judge a launch site.
